Here's the Money Minute.

Most of us treat saving like leftovers. We get paid, the debit orders go off, the groceries get bought, the petrol goes in, and whatever's still standing at the end of the month, *that's* what we save. The problem is, there's almost never anything left. Month-end has a way of eating everything.

So here's the idea: pay yourself first.

It just means flipping the order. Your savings becomes the first bill you pay, not the last. Even a small amount, set aside the moment your salary lands, before the month gets its hands on it.

Think of it as a debit order to yourself. You don't forget to pay the bank, and you don't have to feel motivated for it to happen. It just goes off.

You then live on what's left, and you'll be surprised how often you adjust without even noticing.
