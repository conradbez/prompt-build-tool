# Episode Flow: The Money Minute (Plus Four), about 5 minutes

The source doesn't spell out the segment structure, so this is my interpretation. I read the name as a one-minute core idea plus four minutes of support, and built the sections from the "What needs to change" list and the "one small action" ending.

| Time | Section | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:30 | **1. Hook** | The host opens with a relatable South African money moment, such as a fuel price hike, a high electricity bill, or month-end stress. Occasionally this is tied to a timely event like the monthly fuel announcement or a rand move. The host's voice and personality set the tone, with light code-switching only if it feels natural. |
| 0:30-1:30 | **2. The Money Minute** | This is the core idea in plain language, one concept per episode (for example, what a tax-free savings account is, or why small debts snowball). It should work even if the listener stops here. |
| 1:30-3:30 | **3. The "Plus Four" deep dive** | The host expands on the idea with a real SA scenario in rands, naming local banks, products, or situations. It might include a quick worked example, a common mistake, or a side-hustle angle. Most of this content is evergreen, so it can be batch-recorded. |
| 3:30-4:30 | **4. One small action** | The host gives a single, specific thing to do today, such as "check your credit score" or "open a savings pocket and move R50." This is the retention hook and the most shareable moment. |
| 4:30-5:00 | **5. Close** | The host adds a short "not financial advice" note, invites listeners to follow and forward the episode on WhatsApp (using a short link or UTM for tracking), and teases the next episode. |

## Production notes
- **Timely episodes:** Swap in a news hook at the opening (section 1) and tie the Money Minute to it, while keeping the deep dive evergreen.
- **Tagging for analysis:** Label each episode by topic, hook style, length, and language mix. This lets you compare 30-second retention, average percentage listened, shares, and follower conversion.
- **Drop-off check:** Watch where listeners leave. If they leave before section 4, the action ending isn't doing its retention job.
