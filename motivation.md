What would it say about a map?

Like a street map.

A map of software architecture with unit tests and automated CI/CD integration tests.

I was once walking back from a pub in Dublin, and a friend and I were talking. I was using Google Maps, but due to the conversation and a couple of beers, I got turned around and started following the "this is where you have walked" part of the map. We only realised because we ended up back at the pub. I wish I'd checked my intuition along the way instead of blindly following the map. Similarly, I think when people develop software, they should be thinking about "landmarks", "directionality" and "smoke tests" to test direction and accuracy along the way.

So, two distinct points: we need to work off a map, and we need to validate our map's effectiveness against the real world.
